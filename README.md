In the game, you will control your character's movement and actions within a 3D browser-like environment. The objective is to reach the exit while avoiding obstacles and competing with other players.



How to Play (Controls and Objective)


To play Sprinter, use the following controls:
• Use the left and right arrow keys to navigate your character through the virtual world.
• Use the up arrow key to climb and jump on certain objects.
• Use the down arrow key to descend and move through obstacles.
